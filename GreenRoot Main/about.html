Rooted in Community, Growing a Greener Future

GreenRoot Urban Gardens was founded to bring sustainable growing, education, and fresh food to Pretoria's rooftops and neighborhoods.

Our Mission

We create accessible urban gardens that improve air quality, provide fresh food, and teach sustainable living at every step.

Our Impact

By partnering with schools, businesses, and community groups, we deliver meaningful, measurable environmental and social results.

Meet the Team

Our network of growers, educators and designers works together to make sustainable urban agriculture practical and inspiring.

Why GreenRoot?

We envision every urban roof and courtyard as a place where people can grow, learn, and connect with nature.

We prioritize resilience, community ownership, and design that supports both people and the planet.

From assessment to installation and training, our work is collaborative, evidence-based, and long term.

Core Values

Sustainability

We design resilient gardens that conserve water, restore soil, and reduce food miles.

Education

We teach practical gardening skills, healthy eating, and urban stewardship.

Inclusion

We build accessible spaces where everyone can learn, volunteer, and benefit.
