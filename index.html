Our Mission

Founded in pretoria by Wanga Mamphodo, GreenRoot Urban Gardens cultivates sustainable urban ecosystems by transforming underutilized rooftops into productive community farms.

Key benefits

Sustainability

Reducing carbon footprints through local farming.

Education

Teaching "Green Skills" to the next generation.

Community

Providing fresh produce to local shelters.
